# Mannerbund

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Confirmed: React, TypeScript, Vite. React Router, Zustand, Dexie/IndexedDB, MapLibre and OpenFreeMap. Local prototype; native mobile is a later Expo application sharing domain rules.

## Users

People discovering and gathering with local communities; community owners organizing events and moderating their spaces. Desktop prototype with mobile-first interactions, in Brazilian Portuguese.

## Product Purpose

Turn time spent together in real places into community territory, recognition and enduring event trophies. The complete demonstration goes from discovery and membership to event attendance, territorial conquest and a badge.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Monthly seasons in America/Sao_Paulo. Territories are shared points with a radius. Sum valid person-time; strictly exceeding the incumbent changes ownership; ties preserve the incumbent.
- One active presence per user. Event and free visits count. No unobserved background time. Event badges require RSVP and admin-defined minutes inside the event window.
- Free: one owned community plus one other community. Demo Premium removes both limits; deactivation preserves existing memberships.
- Local accounts/personas, channels, two-person DMs, chronological feed, comments, likes, follows, profiles and community moderation.
- One minute equals one point. Each community has a fictional R$1,000 monthly pool, apportioned by person-time. No payments, real authentication, cloud sync or GPS antifraud in this prototype.
- IndexedDB retains local records and images. Six demo communities and eight São Paulo points. Reset requires confirmation.

## Brand Commitments

Bronze Age material language: aged bronze, dark stone, subtle cartographic details and medallion trophies. Palette and Manrope approved in the implementation plan. Name Mannerbund is provisional. Familiar controls and plain Portuguese take priority over lore.

## Evidence on Hand

No original photos, endorsements or live activity supplied. All people, community activity and rewards are clearly demonstration content. Geographic places are real; demo communities do not claim ownership of them.

## Product Principles

1. Show how territory is calculated, alongside its participants.
2. Reward presence; never manufacture points from messages or likes.
3. Keep historical contributions and awarded trophies immutable.
4. Make community customization simple and reversible.
5. Separate UI, domain rules and persistence for future server-authoritative validation.
